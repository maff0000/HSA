Voice note from a discretionary XAUUSD trader, transcribed verbatim.
15m chart, London session.

The setup starts when I get a large 15m rejection wick. Price runs out to a
new extreme, gets thrown back, and the bar shuts with most of its range left
behind as wick. That says to me the extreme was not accepted.

I do not act on the wick on its own. What I want next is a subsequent
no-wick directional confirmation on the same 15m chart, running the other
way to the wick. The body has to carry the whole bar with nothing hanging
off either end. If that second bar has not printed within four 15m bars of
the wick, I bin the idea and wait for another wick.

Once those two are in, I will drop to the 5m chart and look for a range
breakout to time the entry. That step is optional. If the 5m never gives me
one I still take the setup on the 15m pair alone.

I only ever read this on bar close, never intrabar. The setup stays live for
three 15m bars after the second bar prints, then it is stale and I leave it.
